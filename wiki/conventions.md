# Conventions

The normative rules for notes in this wiki (the shape every note/agent follows). Full rationale
lives in `docs/design/PERSONAL_OS_DESIGN_v2.md` §10.1.

## Frontmatter (every note)
`type`, `title`, `status`, `created`, `updated`, `tags`, `aliases`. Bump `updated:` on every edit.
Allowed `type`: `client | project | area | person | tool | note | runbook | skill | decision |
meeting | research | prediction`.

## Slugs & files
- Filenames are stable, human-readable slugs (`stripe-webhook-retries.md`), **globally unique**
  across folders — because `[[bare-slug]]` links resolve by basename, two notes sharing a basename
  make every link to it ambiguous.
- One idea per atomic note (`notes/`). Entity notes (`clients/`, `projects/`, `areas/`) are *hubs*.

## Links
- Link generously with `[[wikilinks]]`; `[[slug#heading]]` and `[[slug|Display]]` are supported.
- Backlinks are generated by the system, never hand-typed.

## Hubs (two zones)
Client/project/area notes carry a **compiled-truth** top (current synthesis, rewritten in place)
over an append-only `## Timeline` (evidence trail). The top stays short and current; the timeline
never loses history.
